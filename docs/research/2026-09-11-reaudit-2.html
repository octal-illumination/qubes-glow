F1-F3 — coordinate-space errors (corrects previous audit’s M2)

Evidence chain from the pinned KWin 5.27.8 source:

  • createProjectionMatrix(rect, scale) = ortho(rect.left()*scale, ...) (itemrenderer.cpp:45-50) — the vertex/projection layer is device px; pre-multiplying frame/extents/clip-rects by renderTargetScale() at upload is CORRECT (kittyglow.cpp already does this).
  • Scene::addRepaint intersects with the logical viewport, no scaling (scene.cpp:92-101); the GL scissor maps logical->render-target internally via mapToRenderTarget (itemrenderer_opengl.cpp:328-331); PaintData::toMatrix(deviceScale) stores translation in LOGICAL px and applies * deviceScale only when building the matrix (kwineffects.cpp:204-208); blur consumes xTranslation() unscaled in shape math (blur.cpp:628-629). All effect-facing regions (damage, repaints, occluders, halo rect, translations) are LOGICAL.

Therefore the rule is: one scale boundary — at the vertex upload. The previous audit’s M2 (“damage must widen in device px”) confused the two layers. F1/F2/F3 apply the rule:

  • F1 — prePaintWindow and repaintHalo widen damage by maxExtent() * renderTargetScale() (the M2 “fix”): wrong space. Over-widens at s>1 (benign direction; identical at s=1). LL-028 as recorded is WRONG.
  • F2 — occludedAbove() builds occluder rects * scale but they are intersected/subtracted in LOGICAL space and re-scaled at upload: double-scale at s>1 -> over-clip (halo hidden). Benign at s=1. Real latent bug, opposite direction of the M2 claim.
  • F3 — tr = (data.xTranslation() * s, ...): translation is logical; the * s double-scales it at s>1 (once here, once via geo upload) and pollutes the logical halo rect. Benign at s=1.

M5 — rollback leaves kglowsync alive (functional gap)

scripts/kittyglow-rollback.sh unloads/removes the RETIRED kitty-toggle-border script (purged in audit M1) but never touches kglowsync: no unloadScript, no kglowsyncEnabled deletion, no script-dir removal. After a rollback the poller stays loaded, keeps its last desired (borderless) and clientAdded keeps stripping borders from NEW windows — breaking the script’s “working desktop” promise. Fix: rollback must strip kglowsync exactly as it strips kittyglow.
